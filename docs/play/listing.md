## Full description (4000 max)

```
Tafra (طفرة) is your next leap: a weekly training plan, guided workouts and a meal plan that fits your goal, in one free app. No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.

TRAIN
• A weekly plan from ready-made routines (Upper/Lower, Push/Pull/Legs and more), with your training days and times.
• Guided workouts: the next set is ready with a suggested weight, a big Log set button in thumb reach, and a rest timer that keeps running in the notification bar.
• Swap an exercise for a close match, see which muscles it works, and follow form notes and photos.
• Home, cardio, padel, stretching and recovery sessions too.

EAT
• Calorie and protein targets from your profile and goal: lose fat, keep, or build muscle.
• A high-protein meal plan with ticks for each meal, a water counter and your own foods.
• A weekly shopping list made from your plan.
• Ramadan mode with suhoor and iftar times.

SEE PROGRESS
• Weight trend with a safe pace, body measurements and progress photos (kept only on your phone, with location removed).
• Strength records, muscle volume and recovery, weekly streaks with a free skip, and badges that never reward weight loss alone.
• Optional Health Connect: bring in weight from your scale, steps, sleep and resting heart rate from your watch, and send finished workouts back.

SAFE AND PRIVATE
• A short safety check at setup switches off a calorie deficit or changes targets when it should.
• No server and no account: your data never leaves your phone unless you export or share it.
• Backups you can save anywhere, an optional automatic backup, and Delete everything in one place.

Tafra gives general fitness and nutrition guidance and isn't medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new diet or training plan, especially if you're pregnant, have a medical condition or take medication.
```
