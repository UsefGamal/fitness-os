Update Android System WebView

Your data is safe on this phone. Nothing was deleted.

Fitness OS draws its screens with Android System WebView, and the one on this phone is too old (the app needs version 111 or newer).

  1. Open the Play Store.
  2. Search for Android System WebView and tap Update. Update Chrome too if it offers.
  3. Close Fitness OS fully and open it again.

Don't uninstall Fitness OS: that deletes your data.
