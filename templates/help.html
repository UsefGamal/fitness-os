How This Works

A quick reference for what everything on this app means.

Meal Plan
  • The plan runs on a 14-day (2-week) rotation starting Aug 1, 2026 — day 15 loops back to day 1.
  • Every meal has 2 options (A: Egyptian, B: Syrian/grilled) macro-matched to the same daily band — pick whichever you're eating that day, and it's marked "eaten."
  • Day 14 of every cycle is an automatic refeed day — higher-carb, higher-calorie, same food-quality standards. No action needed, it just rotates in.
  • Each meal has an hour window it's meant to fit in, and a "eating out?" panel with swap suggestions if you're not cooking.
  • The hydration meter tracks a 3L/day goal — tap the quick-add buttons as you drink.
  • The shopping list (More → Shopping List) covers both options for the next 7 days, so you have what you need either way.
Training
  • The gym program is a 4-day Upper/Lower split (Upper A → Lower A → Upper B → Lower B → repeat) — no fixed weekly schedule, it just advances one step each time you log a Gym or Home session.
  • Gym and Home share the same rotation counter, so switching between them mid-week doesn't reset the split. Recovery, Cardio, and Padel don't advance it.
  • RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion, 1-10) is how hard a set/session felt — 10 = couldn't do another rep, 7 = a couple reps left in the tank. Logging it lets the weight suggestions adjust automatically (e.g. hold weight if you hit reps but RPE was still 9-10).
  • The app flags a deload reminder once it's been 6+ weeks since your last deload session (checkbox when starting a gym/home session).
  • The body map on each session shows which muscle groups that day's program targets. Each exercise has a one-click video-search link and same-muscle-group alternatives.
  • /gym/progress shows a strength trend per exercise, session volume over time, and your RPE trend.
Body Log
  • Weight is the only required field — waist, chest, arm, hips, thigh, and sleep hours are all optional but useful, especially since the goal is recomposition, not just weight loss (the scale alone can be misleading).
  • Your starting weight (set in Settings) is what the confidence score and predicted goal date are measured against.
Confidence, Predictions & What-If
  • The confidence score blends your actual weight-loss pace (60%) with training + meal adherence (40%) into one number, labeled On Track / Slightly Behind / At Risk.
  • The predicted goal date is a straight-line projection from your recent weigh-in trend — it moves as your real trend does, it's not a fixed countdown.
  • The What-If Simulator (More → What-If Simulator) lets you test how missed sessions, lower meal adherence, a rate change, or a pause would shift that predicted date — separate from the live dashboard numbers, so you can explore scenarios without affecting anything.
Points, Levels & Streaks
  • Quests (Quests tab) are optional bonus tasks — completing one adds points.
  • Level = 1 + total points ÷ 50, no cap. Badges unlock at point thresholds (bronze → silver → gold → emerald → diamond).
  • Streak counts consecutive days with at least one training session logged (any type) — logging nothing today doesn't break it until the day is over.
Padel
  • Logging a result (win/loss/draw) builds your season W-L-D record.
  • If you note discomfort in 3+ of your last 5 sessions (any training type, not just padel), a flag shows up on the dashboard and on the Padel page — just a nudge to pay attention, not a diagnosis.
